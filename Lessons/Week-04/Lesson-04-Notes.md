## ✨ Key Vocabulary & High-Impact Expressions

| Expression / Term | Meaning / Nuance | Context from Lesson |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A run of bad luck** | A series of unfortunate events happening one after another. | *"It was just a run of bad luck."* |
| **Insoluble / Organic solvents** | Unable to dissolve (e.g., azelaic acid in water) vs. carbon-based dissolving liquids. | Formulating a smooth 15–20% azelaic acid moisturizer. |
| **Omit the truth** | Leave out inconvenient facts without telling an outright lie. | Brands claiming "10% azelaic acid" when using a diluted compound. |

| **Avert one's gaze** | Politely look away to avoid staring or making someone uncomfortable. | *"I would avert my gaze all the time."* |
| **Extrapolate** | Extend conclusions from one known situation to a broader pattern. | Inferring someone's priorities from their obsession with status. |
| **Put together** *(adj.)* | Neat, polished, and intentional in appearance. | *"When their eyebrows aren't brushed... I think of them as not put together."* |
| **Full-blown** | Complete, extreme, or fully developed. | *"Not like full-blown to the office."* |
| **Silhouette / Proportions** | The overall outline and visual balance of an outfit on the body. | Preferring an **hourglass** balance over an oversized **reverse/inverted triangle**. |
