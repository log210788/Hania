## 🎯 Goals
1. Drop sounds naturally (**elision**).
2. Hold repeated consonants (**gemination**).
3. Speak for 30 seconds with no translating.
4. Recycle Week 1 phrases (*to be fair*, *quite frankly*, *not quite my cup of tea*).

## 🔇 Elision (sounds that disappear)
| Word | Careful | Natural |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| next day | /nekst deɪ/ | /neks deɪ/ |
| friendship | /ˈfrendʃɪp/ | /ˈfrenʃɪp/ |
| postman | /ˈpəʊstmən/ | /ˈpəʊsmən/ |
| Wednesday | /ˈwednesdeɪ/ | /ˈwenzdeɪ/ |
| handbag | /ˈhændbæɡ/ | /ˈhæmbæɡ/ |

## 🔗 Gemination (hold it once)
*hot tea • black coat • big girl • good day • some milk • take care*
Never add a vowel between the two sounds.

## 🏝️ Survival Kit: 15 Items
Water filter • Fishing net • Waterproof tarpaulin • First-aid kit • Box of matches • Signal mirror • Machete • Strong rope • Cooking pot • Sleeping bag • Sun cream • Hand-crank radio • Compass & map • Tin of biscuits • Ukulele

## 🎮 The 7 Games
1. **Island Survival**: pick 5 of 15; random disasters test your stats.
2. **Defend Your Choices**: 30-second talk with a required phrase and twist.
3. **Elision Detective**: which sound disappears?
4. **Hold It!**: gemination and linking.
5. **The Polite Verdict**: the most British response.
6. **Rescue Radio**: 3-second definitions; reach 10 signal to be rescued.
7. **Island Council**: debate dilemmas.

Badges and stars are collected on the home tab.
